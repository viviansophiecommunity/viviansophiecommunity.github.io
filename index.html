Essential Author Resource

What Is the Goodreads Librarians Group?

The Goodreads Librarians Group is a dedicated community of experienced, volunteer book catalogers who maintain and organize book records across Goodreads. Because Goodreads relies on clean metadata to recommend titles and group reviews together, this community acts as the backbone of book accuracy on the platform.

Why This Community Is Important for Authors

1. Accurate Book Listings & Editions

Librarians add missing books, update cover art, fix broken ISBNs, and merge duplicate editions so all your reader ratings and reviews stay combined in one place.

2. Proper Series & Author Attribution

If your book is part of a series or attached to the wrong author profile with a similar name, the Librarians Group ensures readers find your exact catalog cleanly.

3. Stronger Reader Trust & Discovery

When readers click on a book link and see complete descriptions, accurate page counts, and up-to-date editions, they are far more likely to add your book to their "Want to Read" shelf.
